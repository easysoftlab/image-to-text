## English

### Overview
**Image to Text OCR Converter** is a lightweight, browser-based optical character recognition tool that runs entirely on your local device. It lets you extract editable text from images, screenshots and photos without uploading any files to external servers. All recognition happens directly in your browser — fast, private and completely free.

### ✨ Key Features
- **100% Offline & Private** — All OCR processing runs client-side in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server. Your files and data stay on your device at all times.
- **Automatic Language Detection** — Auto-detects the language of text in your image for optimal recognition accuracy.
- **Multi-Language Support** — Supports a wide range of common and additional languages for text recognition.
- **Smart Text Spacing** — Intelligently restores text spacing and layout for clean, readable output.
- **Mark Text Regions** — Manually mark specific regions on the image to run targeted OCR on exactly the area you need.
- **Real-Time Recognition Stats** — Shows image size, number of text blocks and elapsed time during recognition.
- **Free & No Watermark** — Completely free to use, no registration, no file limits, no hidden costs and no watermarks on output.
- **Cross-Platform** — Works on Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and Android. No installation required — just open the website in any modern browser.
